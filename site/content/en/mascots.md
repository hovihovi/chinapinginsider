Every penguin here carries the jersey, the hairstyle and the playing gestures of one player. No face is reproduced. A mascot that looked like a person would need an image right, a penguin that plays like them does not.

The characters are drawn at home, locally, in one locked style, so the family stays coherent: only the traits of the player change. Where the playing hand or the grip is not confirmed by two independent profile sources, the pose stays neutral and claims nothing about it.
